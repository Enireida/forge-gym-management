Help & Support

Welcome to Forge, the gym management platform. Here are some quick tips:

📋 Members
Add, edit, renew memberships and assign RFID cards from the Members page.
🚪 Door Kiosk
Open the kiosk on a tablet at the entrance. Members tap their card or enter their PIN to check in.
📊 Reports
View revenue trends, top spenders, and member status breakdowns in the Reports section.
