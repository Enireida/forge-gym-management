04

Door kiosk

A tablet at the entrance. Members tap their RFID card and the door auto-toggles check-in or check-out. No staff member is needed at the door. Manual 4-digit PIN entry is available as a backup.

localhost:8080/kiosk

Hold a card to the reader.

Live feed, currently inside count, check-in or check-out auto-detected per scan.

Wearable sync

An open REST API that accepts workout data from Samsung Health, Google Fit, or any device that can send HTTP requests. UUID deduplication makes sync retries safe.

09

AI Coach

Personalized fitness assistant built on the Anthropic Claude API. Before every reply, the system gathers the member's recent workouts, food log, and body measurements so the advice fits them, not a generic average.

localhost:8080/client/ai
Should I add more protein? I feel tired after sessions.
Looking at your last 7 days, you averaged 89g of protein with workouts burning about 320 kcal each. For your weight (68 kg) and lifting frequency, try going up to 110 to 130g. Add a snack 1 to 2 hours after training; Greek yogurt with nuts works well. Your sleep has also been short, and that matters more than people think.
Thanks. Can you build me a plan for this week?
Yes. Based on your measurements (height 168, weight 68) and your last PT session notes from Klara, here is a 4-day split focused on lower body and core.
